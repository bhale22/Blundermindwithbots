Privacy Policy

Covers blundermindchess.com, buildabotchess.com and the Blundermind Android app.
Published by Kelpdog · Effective 3 August 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts. You never give us a name, email or password to play.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking of any kind.
  • There is no database. We have nowhere to keep personal information, and we don't.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information. There is nothing to sell.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your saved bots, your board and piece styles, and which tips you've dismissed are kept in your browser's local storage. The Maia neural network — about 44 MB — is cached on your device so the app works offline and doesn't re-download every time you play.

All of it stays on your device. We cannot read any of it. Clearing the site's data in your browser, or uninstalling the app, removes it permanently.

Playing against the computer

The chess engines run entirely on your device. Your moves are not sent anywhere when you play a bot, and the app works with no network connection at all.

Playing another person online

A two-player game needs a server to pass moves between you. While a game is running we hold the room in memory only: the game code, whose turn it is, and the two connections.

  • Moves and chat messages are relayed straight to your opponent and never written to disk.
  • The room is deleted when both players leave. Abandoned rooms are cleared automatically.
  • Nothing survives a server restart. There is no game history and no chat archive.

Chat is not moderated or reviewed, because it is not stored. Please treat anything you type as visible to the person you're playing.

The openings database

Master-game opening statistics come from the Lichess opening explorer. Your app never contacts Lichess directly — our server makes that request on your behalf and passes the answer back. Only the chess position travels upstream; your IP address and identity are never shared with Lichess.

IP addresses

To stop the openings lookup being abused, our server counts recent requests per IP address, held in memory for a short window and then discarded. It is not written to a database, not linked to anything about you, and not used for any other purpose.

Our host, Railway, may keep standard short-lived request logs as part of running the service, as any web host does. That is infrastructure operation and is governed by their privacy policy.

Children

These apps are suitable for all ages and collect no personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect data from children because we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct or delete personal data all depend on us holding some. We don't. The data that exists is on your device and entirely under your control — clear your browser data or uninstall the app and it is gone. If you would like to confirm any of this, please get in touch.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Material changes to what the app does with data will be described here rather than quietly reworded.
